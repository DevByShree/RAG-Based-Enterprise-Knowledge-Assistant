Q1. Concept of Software Quality and SQA. 
Importance in development 
Software Quality 
Software quality refers to the degree to which software: 
 
Meets functional and non-functional requirements  
 
Is reliable, efficient, maintainable, and user-friendly  
 
Is free from defects  
 
Software Quality Assurance (SQA) 
SQA is a systematic approach to ensure quality throughout the software development life 
cycle (SDLC). 
 
Focuses on process improvement  
 
Prevents defects rather than just detecting them  
 
Importance of SQA 
1. Reduces defects early  
2. Improves reliability and performance  
3. Enhances customer satisfaction  
4. Reduces maintenance cost  
5. Ensures standardization  
6. Improves team productivity

Q6. Statistical Software Quality Assurance 
(SSQA) 
SSQA uses statistical techniques to measure and improve quality. 
Steps 
1. Collect defect data  
2. Classify defects  
3. Analyze frequency  
4. Identify high-risk areas  
5. Improve processes  
 
Benefits 
 
Data-driven decisions  
 
Identifies critical modules  
 
Reduces defect rates  
👉 Example: If most bugs occur in one module → improve that module.

Q10. Pareto Diagram and its role 
Definition 
A Pareto diagram is a bar chart showing problems in decreasing order of importance. 
Principle 
 
Based on 80/20 rule  
 
80% problems come from 20% causes  
 
Role 
 
Identifies critical issues  
 
Helps prioritize problem-solving  
 
Improves quality efficiently  
👉 Example: Most bugs come from few modules → focus on them.
